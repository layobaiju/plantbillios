Plantbill is billing software for plant nurseries in India: shop staff make a customer bill in a few taps, print the receipt, and see the day's sales, cash, expenses and unpaid dues. Users are nursery owners, managers and counter salespeople.

GUIDELINE 1.5 - SUPPORT URL
Corrected. It is now https://sites.google.com/view/plantbill-contact - public, no sign-in, with our WhatsApp, phone and email, and how to get an account, reset a password, print, or have data deleted.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) - BUSINESS MODEL
Nothing is sold in Plantbill or outside it: no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no paid content, no feature unlocked by payment, no advertising.
1. No subscriptions exist. The users are one shop's own staff - owner, manager, salespeople - on accounts we create for that business.
2. Nowhere. There is no store, checkout or payment page for Plantbill anywhere.
3. None. No purchase precedes or accompanies access.
4. None. Every feature works for any signed-in shop: billing, printing, sales and cash book, dues, customers, staff, expenses, reports, offline copies. No tiers, trials, upgrade prompts or purchase links. The app processes no payments - the scan-to-pay screen only draws a QR of the shop's own UPI ID, so the customer pays the shop from their own banking app.
5. It is business software provided to a business - a plant nursery - for its own staff, never sold to consumers, individuals or families; an individual cannot obtain an account. We charge shops nothing at present. Any future fee would be agreed directly with the business owner and settled off the app, never through a consumer storefront.
6. The shop contacts us by WhatsApp, phone or email and we create its account and first login. No fee. There is no sign-up inside the app by design, as an account belongs to one business and its own plant catalogue. The shop's manager then creates staff logins under More > Salespeople.
