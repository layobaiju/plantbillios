WHAT THE APP IS
Plantbill is billing software for plant nurseries and garden shops in India: staff make a customer bill in a few taps, print or share the receipt, and see the day's sales, cash, expenses and unpaid dues. Users are nursery owners, managers and counter salespeople, many of them 50-75 years old, so the app uses large text, one clear action per screen and five Indian languages.

BUSINESS MODEL (no in-app purchase)
Nothing is sold in the app or outside it: no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no paid content, no feature unlocked by payment, no advertising. Every feature works for any signed-in shop. Plantbill is business software provided to a business - a plant nursery - for its own staff; it is not sold to consumers or families, and there is no store or checkout anywhere. Shops are charged nothing at present; any future fee would be agreed directly with the business owner and settled off the app. The "scan to pay" screen only draws a QR of the shop's own UPI ID, so the shop's customer pays the shop from their own banking app; no money passes through the app or through us.

SIGNING IN
There is no public sign-up: each shop's account is created for it by us, because an account belongs to one business and its own plant catalogue. Demo accounts with sample data, one per account type:
Salesperson (counter staff): «email» / «password»
Manager (products, staff, expenses, approvals): «email» / «password»

TRYING THE CORE FEATURES (as the salesperson)
1. Bill tab: search "rose", or tap the microphone and say a plant name.
2. Tap a plant; its saved price fills in. Type the quantity or use +.
3. Optional: "+ Add item" for more plants; a flat or percentage discount.
4. Optional: customer name and 10-digit phone number.
5. Choose Cash, UPI or Split; type an amount in "Due" for a part payment.
6. "Save bill", then "Print receipt" for the AirPrint sheet.
7. Sales tab: day total, cash/UPI/due, cash in hand, expenses, every bill; Dues; Reports (charts + .xlsx).
8. Customers tab; More tab (language, shop details, labour, borrowings, printer, support).
9. Sign in as the manager for Products (add, edit, photo, .csv import) and More > Salespeople.

PERMISSION PROMPTS
Microphone and speech recognition: only for the optional spoken plant search. Bluetooth: only if a shop connects a Bluetooth Low Energy receipt printer.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT HAVE
No account registration or deletion in the app (accounts are created and removed by us or the shop's manager), no purchases or subscriptions, no user-generated public content, no tracking prompt.

OUTSIDE SERVICES
Our own server at https://api.plantbill.in (FastAPI + PostgreSQL, self-hosted in India) holds all shop data. Apple's Speech framework for voice search. AirPrint for printing; Core Bluetooth for an optional BLE receipt printer. Optionally, and only server-side, a shop can have receipts sent through the official WhatsApp Business Platform. No analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, AI or payment SDKs; no external dependencies.

REGIONS
Identical behaviour everywhere; content is India-specific (rupees, UPI, Indian phone numbers, Asia/Kolkata day boundary). Interface in English, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam and Tamil.
